The Journey Begins

A Young Knight. An Ancient Destiny.

Long ago, darkness was sealed away by an ancient guardian. But the seal has weakened, and monsters have begun spreading across the kingdom once again.

A young knight discovers the guardian deep within forgotten ruins. Seeing courage within the hero, the guardian grants him a mysterious magical weapon.

Armed with both steel and magic, the knight must travel across four corrupted worlds, recover ancient artifacts, defeat powerful monsters, and face the Dark Lord before the kingdom falls forever.
